Ibiko v1's built-in encyclopedia (the *.ibk files in this folder, and the fact tables in facts/)

Source: Simple English Wikipedia (https://simple.wikipedia.org), the dump of 1 November 2026
(simplewiki-20261101-pages-articles.xml.bz2, https://dumps.wikimedia.org/simplewiki/).

What is kept: the opening paragraphs of each article, as plain text (templates, references, tables and markup taken
out, cut at a sentence end), and the redirects as other names for an article. Made with tools/build_encyclopedia.py
and coded with tools/encode_encyclopedia.py. The fact tables (facts/) keep the plain facts of each article's
information box (dates, places, figures, names), made with tools/build_facts.py.
