Words and meanings from Wiktionary

knowledge/lexicon_more.tsv(.ibk) and knowledge/language/expressions.tsv(.ibk) hold meanings taken from
Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org), by its contributors, through the machine-readable extract at
kaikki.org (Tatu Ylonen, "Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured Data", LREC 2022).

Wiktionary's text is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and the GNU Free Documentation License. The meanings were
shortened (the first few senses of each word kept) and put in the dictionary's columns; those files are
shared under the same licence.

The Nigerian and Pidgin sayings in expressions.tsv whose meanings Wiktionary lacked were written for Ibiko.

Built by tools/build_lexicon_more.py and tools/build_expressions.py.
